# How learnNow works

## Learning
- **Paths** are courses (for example Core Java, Spring Boot, SQL). A path is made of **topics**, and each topic of **lessons**.
- **Career tracks** are ordered sets of paths for one job, such as Java Full Stack Developer. A track page shows your progress through each path and a Continue button.
- Lessons open in the **study console**. Mark a lesson complete when you finish it. When every lesson in a topic is complete, the topic completes automatically.
- Many lessons end with a short **quiz**: multiple choice, true/false or fill in the blank. Only your first answer to each question earns points.
- Each topic has an **Interview** tab with real interview questions for that topic, with follow-up questions and model answers.
- **DSA practice** is a sheet of coding problems with a built-in editor that runs your code. The **compiler** page runs code in several languages and lets you share a snippet by link.
- **Notes and bookmarks** can be added to lessons and are private to you.

## Points and streaks
- Points: lesson completed 5, topic completed 20 bonus, path completed 100 bonus, correct quiz answer (first attempt) per question value, DSA problem solved 8 / 15 / 25 by difficulty.
- Streak bonuses: 10 points at 3 days, 25 at 7 days, 100 at 30 days.
- A day counts towards your streak when you earn points that day. Missing a whole day resets the current streak; your longest streak is kept.
- The dashboard shows this week's calendar, your streak, points, a weekly leaderboard and a next-step card that opens the exact lesson to continue with.

## The AI trainer (Ask AI)
- Ask AI answers questions about the lesson you are reading, plans what to learn next and advises on interview preparation. You can type or use the microphone.
- It uses your progress, quiz results, streak, study times and your goal to personalise answers. Set or change your goal (target role, target date, hours per week) in the Ask AI panel.
- It keeps short notes about what it learns (for example a topic you find hard). You can see and delete every note from the Ask AI panel.
- Conversations themselves are not stored. Voice recordings are turned into text and not kept.
- Personalisation can be turned off in Privacy settings; Ask AI then answers without using your data.

## Topic interviews (voice practice)
- Practice answering real interview questions out loud with the AI interviewer for ~5 minutes.
- Available on completed topics that have interview questions; you get 5 practice interviews each month.
- Completing an interview earns 15 points, plus a 10-point bonus if your overall score is 80% or higher (awarded once per topic).
- Audio is never stored or recorded. A written transcript and detailed feedback on key points, model answers, and speaking habits are generated in your report.
- Interview records are private, included in your data export, and permanently deleted if you erase your account.

## Account and privacy
- Privacy settings let you download all your data, change consents, name a nominee, raise a grievance, or delete your account. Deleting the account removes your data permanently.
- learnNow is free. Donations ("Buy me a coffee") are optional.
