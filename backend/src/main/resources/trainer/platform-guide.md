# How learnNow works

## Learning
- **Paths** are courses (for example Core Java, Spring Boot, SQL). A path is made of **topics**, and each topic of **lessons**.
- **Career tracks** are ordered sets of paths for one job, such as Java Full Stack Developer. A track page shows your progress through each path and a Continue button.
- Lessons open in the **study console**. Mark a lesson complete when you finish it. When every lesson in a topic is complete, the topic completes automatically.
- Many lessons end with a short **quiz**: multiple choice, true/false or fill in the blank. Only your first answer to each question earns points.
- Each topic has an **Interview** tab with real interview questions for that topic, with follow-up questions and model answers.
- **DSA practice** is a sheet of coding problems with a built-in editor that runs your code. The **compiler** page runs code in several languages and lets you share a snippet by link.
- **Notes and bookmarks** can be added to lessons and are private to you.

## Points and streaks
- Points: lesson completed 5, topic completed 20 bonus, path completed 100 bonus, correct quiz answer (first attempt) per question value, DSA problem solved 8 / 15 / 25 by difficulty.
- Streak bonuses: 10 points at 3 days, 25 at 7 days, 100 at 30 days.
- A day counts towards your streak when you earn points that day. Missing a whole day resets the current streak; your longest streak is kept.
- The dashboard shows this week's calendar, your streak, points, a weekly leaderboard and a next-step card that opens the exact lesson to continue with.

## The AI trainer (Ask AI)
- Ask AI answers questions about the lesson you are reading, plans what to learn next and advises on interview preparation. You can type or use the microphone.
- It uses your progress, quiz results, streak, study times and your goal to personalise answers. Set or change your goal (target role, target date, hours per week) in the Ask AI panel.
- It keeps short notes about what it learns (for example a topic you find hard). You can see and delete every note from the Ask AI panel.
- Conversations themselves are not stored. Voice recordings are turned into text and not kept.
- Personalisation can be turned off in Privacy settings; Ask AI then answers without using your data.

## Topic interviews (voice practice)
- Practise answering real interview questions out loud with the AI interviewer for about 6 minutes.
- Available on completed topics that have interview questions; you get 5 topic interviews each month.
- Completing a topic interview earns 15 points, plus a 10-point bonus if your overall score is 70 or more (awarded once per topic).
- Audio is never stored or recorded. A written transcript and detailed feedback on key points, model answers, and speaking habits are generated in your report.
- The report also scores accuracy, depth, structure and communication, and gives two strengths, two things to improve and one next step. It judges what you said, never your voice, accent or confidence.
- **Retry a question**: from a report you can answer any one question again by voice (about 3 minutes). Up to 5 retries a day; they earn no points and do not use your monthly interviews.
- Each report asks "Was this evaluation fair?". A thumbs down, with an optional comment, is read by the team.
- Interview records are private, included in your data export, and permanently deleted if you erase your account.

## Mock interviews
- Full practice interviews by voice, on the **AI Trainer** page: Resume interview (15 minutes), HR round (10), Behavioural round (10), Project deep-dive (10) and Full interview round (25, everything together).
- Resume, project and full-round interviews need your resume uploaded first. You can type the role you are aiming for and, if you saved a job description, base the interview on it.
- Technical questions come from learnNow's own interview bank where it covers your skills; the rest (your projects, behavioural and HR questions, or other professions) are written for you.
- You get 2 mock interviews each month. Each one earns 25 points, plus 15 if your overall score is 70 or more.

## Resume review
- On the AI Trainer page, upload your resume as a PDF or Word (.docx) file under 5 MB. You must be 18 or older.
- You get an overall score, scores for ATS-friendliness, impact, clarity and skills, strengths, issues by severity, suggested rewrites and missing sections. Rewrites may contain placeholders like [X%]: fill them in with your real numbers, never invented ones.
- You can correct what was read (headline, level, years of experience, target role, skills), and paste a job description to see how well you match it, which keywords you have and which you lack.
- The file itself is never stored, only the text read from it. 3 reviews a month.
- **Delete resume** on the AI Trainer page removes the resume, its review and any job description and match.

## Weekly plan
- On the AI Trainer page the trainer writes a plan for your week (Monday to Sunday): lessons, quizzes, interviews and revision, sized to the hours a week in your goal. Today's tasks also show on the trainer page.
- You can generate it up to 3 times a week. It needs personalisation turned on, because it is built from your progress.

## Readiness
- Readiness shows, per topic you have studied, how ready you are to be interviewed on it: 60% your latest topic-interview score and 40% your quiz accuracy (only once you have answered at least 5 quiz questions on it), or whichever of the two you have.
- Under 55 is weak, under 75 okay, 75 and above strong. A topic with neither is untested.

## Limits
- Each month: 5 topic interviews, 2 mock interviews, 3 resume reviews. Each day: 5 question retries. Each week: 3 weekly plans. Months and days follow your own time zone. An interview that fails to start or is never answered does not count.

## Account and privacy
- Privacy settings let you download all your data, change consents, name a nominee, raise a grievance, or delete your account. Deleting the account removes your data permanently.
- learnNow is free. Donations ("Buy me a coffee") are optional.
